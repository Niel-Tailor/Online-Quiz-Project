How to take the quiz

Quiz How To

Instructions

  1. Choose the number of questions, category, difficulty and question type.
  2. Click Start quiz to load your questions.
  3. Click an answer to select it. It will be highlighted, and you can change it before moving on.
  4. Click Next to go to the next question.
  5. On the last question, click Submit quiz to see your results.
  6. Look at your score and see which answers you got right or wrong.
  7. Click Take quiz again to play another round.

Scoring

Each correct answer is worth 1 point. You do not lose points for wrong answers. Your score is shown as a number and as a percentage.
